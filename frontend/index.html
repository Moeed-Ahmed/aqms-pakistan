ℹ️ What is PM2.5?▾

Fine particles ≤ 2.5µm — 30× thinner than a human hair. They penetrate deep into lungs and bloodstream.

Health Risk

Causes heart disease, lung cancer, stroke, respiratory illness. WHO safe limit: 15 µg/m³ annual mean. Pakistan averages 5–8× this limit in major cities.

Main Sources

Vehicle exhaust, brick kilns, crop burning (Oct–Nov), industrial emissions, temperature inversions trapping smog.

Prevention

N95 masks when AQI > 100, HEPA air purifiers indoors, avoid outdoor exercise on bad days.

ℹ️ What is PM10?▾

Coarser particles ≤ 10µm (dust, pollen, mould). WHO limit: 45 µg/m³ annual mean.

Sources

Road dust, Balochistan desert winds, demolition, agricultural harvesting, cement industries.

Tip

Keep windows closed during dust storms (especially May–Jun in Sindh and summer in Balochistan).

⚠️ Disaster Risks▾

🌊 Floods — Monsoon (Jul–Sep) annual. 2022 floods submerged 1/3 of the country.

⚡ Earthquakes — Balochistan and KPK on active fault lines.

⛰️ Landslides — GB, AJK, KPK hills after heavy rain.

🌀 Cyclones — Arabian Sea, threat to Karachi coast (Oct–Nov).

☀️ Drought — Thar Desert and arid Balochistan water stress.

💨 Wind & Smog▾

Wind particles show real-time flow from Open-Meteo. Strong winds disperse PM2.5; calm winter conditions create temperature inversions trapping pollution — main cause of Lahore's Oct–Jan smog crisis.

Monsoon winds arrive from the Bay of Bengal (Jul–Sep). Dry westerlies dominate Oct–Mar.
